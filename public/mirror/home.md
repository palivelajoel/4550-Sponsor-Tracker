# FRC Team 4550 "Something's Bruin"

## About
FRC Team 4550 "Something's Bruin" has been competing since 2012, representing Cherry Creek High School in FIRST Robotics Competition. Our team of 40–50 student engineers, programmers, and designers builds competition-ready robots each season from scratch in six weeks. We competed at the 2016 World Championship.

- **Years competing:** 12+
- **Members:** 40–50
- **World Championship:** 2016
- **Sub-Teams:** 3

## Sub-Teams
- **Build** — Designs, fabricates, and assembles the competition robot. Members learn CAD, machining, welding, and mechanical engineering.
- **Programming** — Develops autonomous routines, driver controls, sensor integration, and computer vision. Members work in Java with WPILib.
- **Marketing & Outreach** — Handles sponsor outreach, social media, community events, STEM education, and branding.

## Outreach
- Mentoring younger FRC and FLL teams in the Denver metro area
- Visiting local elementary and middle schools for robotics demos
- Participating in STEM fairs, library events, and community festivals
